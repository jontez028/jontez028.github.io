Equity curve (R)
Recent trades

Method: a signal needs the weighted multi-timeframe bias to reach 30 in one direction, then a 20 EMA pullback or a 20-bar breakout, then a confluence score of 55 or more out of 100. Entry is a limit order at the signal bar close and must trade on the next bar. The stop sits beyond the last six-bar swing, limited to 0.8 to 2.5 ATR. A third of the position exits at each of 1R, 2R and 3R. The stop moves to entry after TP1 and to TP1 after TP2. Costs are $0.30 per ounce. When a bar touches both stop and target, the stop counts first. Open trades exit at the close after 48 bars.
