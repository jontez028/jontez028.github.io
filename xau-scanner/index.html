Strategy scoreboard (real gold data, 15m to 4H, costs included)

Rule from your list: profit factor above 1.3 in both halves, positive expectancy after costs and at least 100 trades. Gated set: on real data the combined 15m and 1H results beat the old all-strategies mix (35R against 29R over 7 weeks to 7 months), but the choice was made on the same data. Strategies that lost are switched off and show as off. Under 15m the engine is about break-even after costs, so treat 1m and 5m alerts as unproven. None of the 14 pass yet on the data available (about 2 years of 1H, 30 weeks of 15m, 18 days of 5m, in separate pieces). Signals use the router, a session filter and a confluence score of 3 to stay selective, and every strategy should be paper traded 4 to 8 weeks first.

Method: a router reads ADX(14). Below 20 the market counts as ranging and the mean reversion group runs (RSI(2), Bollinger fade, day-average reversion, range fade) with sweeps and divergence. At 25 or more it counts as trending and the order block, EMA pullback, breakout retest and Fibonacci setups run. Between 20 and 25 the sweep, divergence, spike fade, opening range, Fibonacci and EMA setups run. Each setup needs 3 of 5 confluence points (4H bias, 1H structure, key level or gap, momentum, clean candle) and must fall between 07:30 and 17:00 UTC. The short-term and counter-trend signals from before are kept. Entry is a limit order at the signal bar close and must trade on the next bar. A third exits at each of TP1, TP2 and TP3 priced from the strategy rules, the stop moves to entry after TP1 and to TP1 after TP2, costs are $0.30 per ounce, the stop counts first when a bar touches both, and trades time out after the strategy time limit. No news calendar is connected, so the news blackout is not applied and the spike fade is a pattern rule. Day average uses typical price without volume.
